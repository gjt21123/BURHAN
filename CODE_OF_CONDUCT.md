# Code of Conduct

BURHAN welcomes technical disagreement, security criticism, and rigorous review. Participation must remain professional and focused on improving the project.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- critique ideas, code, evidence, and assumptions rather than people;
- communicate clearly and respectfully;
- disclose relevant uncertainty and conflicts of interest;
- give credit for contributions and prior work;
- handle security reports responsibly;
- avoid publishing private information, credentials, or exploit details without authorization;
- make space for contributors with different levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

The project does not accept:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or targeted humiliation;
- doxxing or disclosure of private information;
- sexualized or abusive conduct;
- deliberate disruption of project spaces;
- knowingly fabricated benchmarks, security claims, evidence, or attribution;
- attempts to pressure contributors into weakening documented safety or verification boundaries without technical justification.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and the project.

For conduct concerns that cannot be handled safely in a public issue, contact the repository maintainer privately through the GitHub profile associated with this project.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented when appropriate, and focused on maintaining a safe, evidence-driven engineering environment.
